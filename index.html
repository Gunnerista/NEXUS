Reverse Match · Case #001

Player in. Destinations out.

Case #001: an Asia based international centre back (left footed, national team starter at the 2026 World Cup) with a six figure release clause and an efficient wage ask. NEXUS inverted the usual logic: with the fee this affordable, the binding filter is not budget — it is which clubs have a genuine, current gap and the will to move this window. Club identities below are anonymized while the deal is live.

How the ranking works

Three axes, in strict priority order: league level and guaranteed minutes, then budget and wage fit, then position and squad gap verified against this week's news. A club must clear all three. Every claim that could not be verified live carries an explicit Verify flag instead of being asserted.

FA Prediction Tracker

Predict. Lock. Score.

For a pool of currently unattached free agents, NEXUS predicts the destination league and top three clubs — then locks the prediction (2026-08-16). When the player actually signs, the system grades itself: 3 points for the exact club, 2 for the right league, 1 for an adjacent market, 0 for a miss. Predictions are never edited after lock.

The loop

A weekly automated scan checks signing news for every tracked free agent → resolves and grades → updates this board. The lock rule is absolute: once a prediction is locked, changing it is forbidden even when new signals appear. New information feeds the next prediction, not the old one.
